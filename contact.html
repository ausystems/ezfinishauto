BEFORE YOU BOOK

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What people ask us most before their first mobile detail in the Greater Toronto Area.

01

Do you come to my home or workplace?

Yes. EZ Finish Auto is a fully mobile detailing service, so we bring the detail to you anywhere in the Greater Toronto Area. Most appointments happen in a driveway, a condo parking garage or an office lot, and you never have to drop the car off or sit waiting at a shop.

02

How much does mobile car detailing cost?

Sedans start at $95, SUVs start at $135, and vans and trucks start at $185. Every package covers the inside and the outside of the vehicle: interior deep cleaning, exterior wash and detail, and wheels and tires. If your vehicle needs extra attention, such as heavy pet hair or set in stains, tell us when you book so we can set aside the time.

03

How long does a full detail take?

A sedan usually takes about two to three hours, and larger SUVs, vans and trucks take longer. We book one vehicle per appointment, so the time is yours and the work is never rushed. You do not need to stay with the car while we detail, so you can carry on with your day.

04

What do you need from me on the day?

A parking spot with a little room to work around the vehicle, roughly the space of one and a half cars, and the keys. Let us know when you book if access to water or power is limited where you park, and we will plan the visit around it.

05

Which cities in the Greater Toronto Area do you serve?

We serve every city in the GTA, including Toronto, North York, Scarborough, Etobicoke, Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, Oakville, Burlington, Ajax, Pickering, Whitby and Oshawa. If you are just outside that list, call or text us and we will tell you right away whether we can reach you.

06

How do I book an appointment?

Call or text +1 647-424-4813, message us on Instagram at @ezfinishauto, or send the booking request on our contact page with your vehicle, your city and the times that suit you. We reply with a confirmed window, and you can text us any time before the appointment if your plans change.
